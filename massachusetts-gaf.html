Massachusetts Gas Adjustment Factors

The gas adjustment factor (GAF) passes each utility’s cost of buying and delivering gas supply through to customers; the local distribution adjustment factor (LDAF) recovers other reconciling costs. Both are filed with the Massachusetts Department of Public Utilities and billed per therm, on top of base distribution rates and customer charges set in each company’s rate case.
