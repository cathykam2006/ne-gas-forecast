Massachusetts Gas Demand Outlook

Monthly natural gas delivered by Massachusetts utilities to homes, businesses and industry (power plants excluded), with a 12-month forecast under normal, cold and warm weather. EIA publishes the actual figures about two months late, so the forecast starts from the latest published month.

The dashed blue line is the forecast with normal weather; the dotted lines are a cold or warm winter (90th and 10th percentile monthly heating degree days over the last 15 years); the band is the likely range from backtest errors at each horizon. Weather sensitivity: about MMcf of extra demand per heating degree day.

Source: EIA natural gas deliveries to Massachusetts residential, commercial and industrial customers; weather from ERA5 via Open-Meteo.

Data: EIA (Henry Hub spot, storage, LNG exports), CME NYMEX via Yahoo Finance (futures), Massachusetts DPU (GAF/LDAF), NOAA (weather). Source code: github.com/cathykam2006/ne-gas-forecast.
